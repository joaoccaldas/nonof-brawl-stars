## 📊 FP&A Context

### Current Tools
| Tool | Purpose | Integration Level | Pain Points |
|------|---------|-------------------|-------------|
| SAP / BW | ERP / reporting / source operational data | Core but fragmented in practice | Hard to access in the right shape; knowledge concentrated in few people; not AI/automation-friendly by default |
| Excel | Analysis / bridging / modeling / customer outputs | Very high | Too much repetitive file-level work; error-prone when used as manual transport layer |

- Net sales
- Product margin / gross margin
- SPC and related cost/per-unit checks
- Bonus accrual levels / customer settlement impact

- FTEs, vacancies, overhead phasing, cost-savings / cost-avoidance initiatives
- Market share / competitive position via GfK and related market data
- Service response time, first-time fix, technician efficiency, RCC incident patterns

### Data Sources
- SAP / BW reports (including delivered-goods and open-order views)
- CRM / SERUM bonus agreement and settlement data
- Excel-based controller models and customer-level files
- Power BI / reporting layers built on top of warehouse/system extracts
